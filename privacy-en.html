Nondori Privacy Policy

Nondori (the "App") respects your privacy and collects no personal information.

1. Information we collect

The App collects no personal information such as your name, email address, or location. No account registration is required.

2. Where your records are stored

Your sobriety records, settings, and other data are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. They are never sent to a developer-operated server.

3. Advertising and analytics

The App shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

4. Payments

In-app purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. The App does not obtain or store payment information such as credit card numbers.

5. Backup

Records are exported and imported only by your own actions. You are responsible for managing any file you export.

6. Sending records to Muse (AI assistant)

The "Send records to Muse" button in Settings and the "I want a drink" button on the Home screen send a summary of your records and your record data (JSON) from your own Mail app to the Muse email address you set — and only when you tap the button yourself. The App never sends anything automatically. Records you send are used by the AI assistant "Muse" for reflection and encouragement. The "I want a drink" button can be turned off in Settings.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us at supportAppiOs+nondori@gmail.com.
