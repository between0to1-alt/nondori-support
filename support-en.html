Nondori Support

Support page for Nondori, the sober grid tracker.

I recorded the wrong day. Can I fix it?

Tap the day you want to change on the Home grid to edit or delete that day's record. Days before your start date and future days cannot be changed.

How do I record a day I drank?

Tap that day and choose "Drank". Your streak resets, but your cumulative count of sober days is kept.

Will my records disappear if I change phones?

If you are signed in to iCloud, your records carry over automatically. Otherwise, use "Export backup" in Settings to save a file, then "Import" on your new device. Backup is free.

I want to restore my Pro purchase.

In Settings, use "Restore purchases" in the Pro section. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account.

I want to cancel the annual plan.

Cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You can keep using Pro until the end of the paid period. The lifetime plan has no cancellation — it lasts forever.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that reminders are turned on in the App's Settings, and that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Nondori.
